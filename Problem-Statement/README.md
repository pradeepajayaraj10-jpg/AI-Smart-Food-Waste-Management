# Problem Statement

## Project Title

AI-Based Smart Food Waste Management System

## Problem

College canteens may prepare food based on estimated demand rather than accurate daily requirements. This can cause excess food preparation and unnecessary food wastage, while inaccurate estimation can also result in food shortages.

## Need

A smarter approach is needed to help canteen staff estimate food requirements according to expected student demand.

## Proposed Direction

The project explores an AI-based solution that can analyse relevant demand patterns and provide food quantity recommendations to support better planning.
