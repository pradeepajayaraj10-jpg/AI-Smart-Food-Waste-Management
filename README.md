## Project Better Tomorrow – Semester 3 C29

### Pathway

## Problem Statement

College canteens often face difficulties in estimating the right quantity of food to prepare each day. Over-preparation can lead to food wastage, while under-preparation can result in food shortages.

## Proposed Solution

The AI-Based Smart Food Waste Management System is designed to help college canteens predict daily food demand and plan food preparation more effectively using data-driven insights.

## Objectives

* Reduce food wastage in college canteens
* Predict daily food demand
* Support better food preparation planning
* Track food consumption and wastage
* Promote efficient use of food resources

AI is used as a divergence partner during the ideation stage to generate, compare, stress-test, and refine possible solutions.
